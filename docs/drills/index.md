# Drills

Drills are how technique becomes instinct. The goal is not to memorise sequences — it is to build **automatic responses** through repetition.

| Drill | Type | Description |
|---|---|---|
| [Sinawali](sinawali.md) | Solo + Partner | Weaving patterns — rhythm and flow |
| [Sombrada](sombrada.md) | Partner | Strike-block-counter flow cycle |
| [Sparring](sparring.md) | Partner | Live application with controlled contact |

---

## General Drilling Principles

1. **Slow before fast.** If you can't do it slow, you can't do it fast correctly.
2. **Both sides.** Every drill, both dominant and non-dominant.
3. **No zombie reps.** Stay mentally present — if your mind wanders, stop and reset.
4. **Film occasionally.** What you feel you're doing and what you're actually doing are often very different.
