# Sinawali — Weaving Patterns

*Sinawali* means "to weave" in Filipino. These are rhythmic, flowing double-stick (or empty-hand) patterns that build coordination, timing, ambidexterity, and flow.

---

## Heaven Six (Anim na Langit)

The most fundamental sinawali pattern. All others are variations of this.

**Striking sequence (from your perspective):**

```
Right-high → Left-high → Right-mid → Left-mid → Right-low → Left-low → (repeat)
```

**Partner feeding pattern:** Your partner mirrors — their Right-high meets your Right-high, and so on. You are simultaneously striking AND receiving at the same rhythm.

<!-- ![Heaven Six Sinawali](../assets/gifs/sinawali-heaven-six.gif) -->

=== "Solo Practice"
    1. Stand in fighting stance
    2. Strike Heaven Six in the air × 3 minutes
    3. Count aloud: "Right, left, right, left..."
    4. Focus: equal power from both hands, consistent rhythm

=== "Partner Practice"
    1. Stand 1 stick-length apart
    2. Begin slowly — establish the rhythm together
    3. Sticks meet at the midpoint between you — neither partner reaches into the other's space
    4. Gradually increase speed as synchrony develops
    5. Do NOT race — if one person speeds up, the pattern breaks

---

## Earth Six

The reverse of Heaven Six — starts low, moves high.

```
Right-low → Left-low → Right-mid → Left-mid → Right-high → Left-high → (repeat)
```

**Use:** Builds comfort striking from low to high angles.

---

## Redondo (Circular Sinawali)

Instead of linear strikes, each hand makes a small inward circle before striking.

- The circle is generated by the **wrist and forearm**, not the shoulder
- Creates a whipping, unpredictable strike path
- Harder to read and intercept than straight sinawali

## Abaniko Sinawali (Fan Sinawali)

Uses the fanning wrist motion (abaniko) on each strike rather than a straight downward arc.

- Compact, close-range pattern
- Each strike "fans" outward then snaps back
- Very fast once the wrist conditioning is built

---

## Training Tips

!!! tip "Find the zone"
    Sinawali has a meditative quality at speed — you stop thinking and just flow. That state is what you're training toward. Don't rush to get there; it comes with reps.

!!! warning "Don't let the off-hand drop"
    The most common sinawali error: the non-dominant hand droops below the ideal strike zone. Both hands stay active at all times.

## Progression Ladder

| Stage | Focus | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Solo Heaven Six, slow | 1 week |
| 2 | Partner Heaven Six, slow | 1–2 weeks |
| 3 | Partner Heaven Six, increase speed | Ongoing |
| 4 | Introduce Earth Six | After Heaven Six is automatic |
| 5 | Combine Heaven + Earth alternating | After both are solid |
| 6 | Redondo solo | Whenever |
| 7 | Redondo partner | After Redondo solo is comfortable |
