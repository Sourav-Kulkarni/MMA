# Sombrada — Umbrella Flow Drill

*Sombrada* (from Spanish: *shadow*) is a partner flow drill where one person feeds a strike, the other blocks and counters, and the pattern continues in a cycle. It is the bridge between drilling and sparring.

---

## Basic Sombrada Cycle

A 3-count exchange:

```
Feeder:    Strike (Angle 1) → Block partner's counter → Strike (Angle 1) → ...
Receiver:  Block → Counter-strike (Angle 1) → Block feeder's strike → ...
```

Both partners are simultaneously attacking and defending, flowing continuously.

## Step-by-Step (First Learn)

**Setup:** Partner A = Feeder, Partner B = Receiver. Standing at stick distance.

1. **A** throws Angle 1 (forehand diagonal, high)
2. **B** executes roof block, redirecting A's stick outward
3. **B** immediately counters with Angle 1
4. **A** executes roof block, redirecting B's stick outward
5. **A** immediately counters with Angle 1 → back to step 2

The drill loops. Neither partner stops to "reset."

---

## The Keys to Good Sombrada

| Principle | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Flow** | No pausing between block and counter — block IS the beginning of your counter |
| **Consistent rhythm** | Like a metronome — don't speed up or slow down mid-drill |
| **Eye contact** | Watch your partner's shoulder/torso, not the stick |
| **Alive hand** | Non-weapon hand should be checking on every exchange |

---

## Adding the Alive Hand Check

Once the basic flow is smooth, add:

- As you block with your stick, your alive hand **checks** the attacker's weapon forearm simultaneously
- This mirrors a real defensive application — checking prevents a second immediate attack

---

## Variations

=== "Two-Count Sombrada"
    Faster, less complex:
    - Feeder strikes
    - Receiver parries AND counters in a single smooth motion (no distinct block-then-counter)
    - Feeder absorbs counter and immediately re-strikes

=== "Angle Variation Sombrada"
    - Start with Angle 1 basic
    - After 10 reps, Feeder switches to Angle 2 (backhand)
    - Receiver adjusts block to match
    - Progress to calling angles randomly

=== "Largo Mano (Long Range) Sombrada"
    - Both partners take one step back
    - Only tip-of-stick contact — no body checked
    - Emphasises footwork: you must step in to strike and step back to avoid the counter

---

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Muscling through the block | The block is a redirect, not a bash — use the stick to guide, not smash |
| Counter comes too late | Block and counter are one motion. Drill: freeze after every block and check your counter hand — is it already moving? |
| Watching the stick | Focus on torso/shoulders — the stick follows the body |
| Same speed always | Vary rhythm in training. Slow → pause → burst. Prepare for unpredictability. |
