## Recommended External Resources

### Books
- *The Secrets of Arnis* — Remy Presas (Modern Arnis founder)
- *Filipino Martial Culture* — Mark Wiley

### YouTube Channels
- Search: **Sayoc Kali**, **Pekiti Tirsia Kali**, **Dog Brothers Martial Arts**, **Modern Arnis** for technique references

### Equipment
- **Sticks:** Rattan from any Filipino martial arts supplier — ~28 inches, 3/4 inch diameter
- **Training knife:** Rubber or hardwood training daga
- **Gloves:** Escrima-specific gloves (not boxing gloves — you need finger articulation)
