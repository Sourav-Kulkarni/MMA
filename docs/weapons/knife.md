# Knife — Daga

!!! danger "Training knives only"
    All knife work is done with **rubber or wooden training knives**. Never train live blade techniques with a real knife until your instructor clears you for supervised practice.

---

## Why Train the Knife?

- Develops acute awareness of **edge and tip** at all times
- Teaches you to respect the danger zone (the opponent's weapon hand)
- Cross-trains beautifully with empty hand and stick

---

## Grip Types

=== "Forward Grip (Filipino Grip)"
    - Blade extends from the thumb side of the fist
    - Edge facing outward (sabre-style)
    - Most versatile for slashing and thrusting

=== "Reverse Grip (Icepick)"
    - Blade extends downward from the pinky side
    - Strong for close-range downward stabs and hooking
    - Limited thrusting range — closer quarters weapon

---

## Edge Awareness Drill

Before any technique: with training knife in hand, identify the **four danger zones** you present to an opponent:

1. Weapon hand / forearm
2. Lead leg / thigh
3. Centreline (stomach/chest)
4. Face / neck

Now identify those same zones on your training partner. This is what Kali calls *reading the opponent's weapons*.

---

## Basic Knife Techniques

### Forehand Slash (Pluma)

- Similar motion to Angle 1 strike with stick
- Cut with the edge, not the flat — rotation of the wrist at contact
- Retract immediately — do not leave the arm extended

### Thrust (Saksak)

- Drive the tip straight into the target
- Alive hand checks the opponent's weapon arm simultaneously
- Short, sharp — not a push

### Angle 6 Upward Slash (Defang)

- Rising cut targeting the opponent's weapon forearm or wrist
- The classic "defang the snake" application
- Executed as a counter after stepping offline

---

## Partner Drill: Touch and Check

1. Both partners hold training knives
2. Slow motion — try to touch your partner's weapon hand
3. Partner checks (intercepts) the attempt with their alive hand
4. Switch attacker/defender roles every 30 seconds
5. No power, no speed — this is a sensitivity drill

---

!!! tip "The alive hand is your armour"
    In knife combat, your non-weapon hand should never be idle. It is always checking, trapping, or protecting. A dangling alive hand is an invitation to be defanged.
