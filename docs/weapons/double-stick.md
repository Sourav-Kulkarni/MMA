# Double Stick — Doble Olisi

Double stick training builds ambidexterity, independent hand coordination, and rhythm. The patterns you learn here are the basis of sinawali drills.

---

## Grip — Both Hands

Use standard grip in both hands. The non-dominant hand will feel awkward initially — this is expected and will pass.

**Critical:** Do not let the non-dominant hand become passive. Both hands are attacking weapons.

---

## Coordination Principle

Double stick movements fall into two categories:

| Pattern | Description |
|---|---|
| **Alternating** | Hands strike in sequence — left, right, left, right |
| **Simultaneous** | Both hands strike at the same moment (high/low, inside/outside) |

Start with alternating patterns. Add simultaneous patterns after coordination is solid.

---

## Basic Double Stick Striking Pattern

**Heaven Six (the foundation of all sinawali):**

```
R-high → L-high → R-mid → L-mid → R-low → L-low
(repeat continuously)
```

- Each strike is Angle 1 (forehand) on the corresponding side
- Keep the off-hand ready — not dropping to your side
- Head stays still; hands do the work

## Drills

=== "Solo"
    - Heaven Six × 3 minutes non-stop
    - Count rhythm aloud: "1-2, 1-2, 1-2"
    - If coordination breaks, slow down — do not muscle through

=== "Partner (feeding)"
    - Partner holds up pads in the matching zones
    - You strike each in sequence
    - Partner varies the rhythm to keep you reactive

---

!!! tip "Brain before speed"
    Double stick is more coordination than conditioning. 5 minutes of slow, deliberate, correct double-stick work is worth more than 20 minutes of sloppy fast reps.
