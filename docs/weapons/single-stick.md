# Single Stick — Olisi / Baston

The single stick is the entry point of Kali. Everything you learn here maps to blade, empty hand, and all other weapons.

**Standard training stick:** Rattan, 28 inches, ~3/4 inch diameter

---

## Core Strikes

### Forehand Strike (Abaniko / Fan Strike)

A fanning, wrist-driven strike primarily targeting the temple, neck, or collarbone.

**Execution:**
1. Hold the stick in standard grip
2. Raise the weapon to roughly ear level (not behind the shoulder — stay tight)
3. Fan the wrist outward — the stick arcs like a pendulum
4. Point of contact: the last 4–6 inches of the stick (the tip section is fastest)
5. Retract immediately along the same arc or redirect to next angle

<!-- ![Abaniko Strike](../assets/gifs/abaniko.gif) -->

!!! tip
    The power in an Abaniko comes from the **wrist snap**, not a full arm swing. A compact, snapping Abaniko is faster and harder to read than a big wind-up.

### Punyo Strike

Using the **butt end** of the stick as the striking surface. A short, sharp close-range weapon.

**When:** After a forehand strike, instead of fully retracting, drive the punyo backward into the target (face, sternum, stomach).

**Range:** Very close — typically used in trapping or grappling range.

## Core Blocks / Parries

=== "Roof Block"
    - Stick held diagonally above the head, tip pointing forward-up
    - Intercepts downward strikes (Angle 1, 2, 12)
    - Weight low, knees bent — you are a structure, not just an arm block

=== "Umbrella Block (Payong)"
    - Stick held overhead, nearly horizontal, palm facing out
    - Covers a wider arc than the roof block
    - Good against incoming Angle 1 with simultaneous step offline

=== "Inside Block"
    - Parry the attacking weapon **inward** (across your centreline)
    - Opens the opponent's outside line for a counter
    - Combine with a triangle step to the outside

=== "Outside Block"
    - Parry the attacking weapon **outward** (away from your centreline)
    - Opens the inside line
    - Aggressive counter follows immediately

## Basic Combinations

### Combination 1: High-Low-Thrust

| Step | Action | Angle |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Forehand diagonal strike | Angle 1 |
| 2 | Backhand diagonal strike | Angle 2 |
| 3 | Thrust to centre | Angle 5 |

**Focus:** Smooth retraction between strikes. Each strike should feel like a drop into the next.

---

### Combination 2: Temple-Rib-Temple

| Step | Action | Angle |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Forehand diagonal (high) | Angle 1 |
| 2 | Forehand horizontal (mid) | Angle 3 |
| 3 | Forehand diagonal (high) | Angle 1 |

**Focus:** Power transfer through rotation. Do not reset stance between strikes.

## Drilling Solo

- [ ] Shadow striking — all 12 angles × 10 reps each

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Telegraphing with shoulder wind-up | Start motion from the wrist and elbow |
| Retracting too slowly | Treat retraction as the next attack, not a rest |
| Standing flat-footed while striking | Step with every strike — connect footwork to weapon work |
| Focusing only on dominant hand | Train left hand daily, even if awkward |
