# Stances & Footwork

Good footwork is what keeps you alive. Your stance controls your balance, your range, your mobility, and your angles of attack.

---

## Core Stances

### 1. Fighting Stance (Depensa)

The default ready position.

**Setup:**
- Feet shoulder-width apart, dominant foot slightly back
- Weight distributed 50/50 or slightly back (60% rear)
- Knees slightly bent — never locked
- Weapon hand forward at roughly chest height
- Non-weapon hand guard up near the chin or cheek

**Key points:**

- Keep your weight on the **balls of your feet**, never your heels
- Shoulders relaxed — tension = slow
- Chin slightly tucked

### 2. Forward Stance (Pasa Forward)

Used for driving power into a strike.

**Setup:**
- Dominant foot forward
- Weight shifted forward (60/40)
- Hips square or slightly angled toward target

**Use case:** Thrusting attacks, closing distance aggressively.

---

### 3. Side Stance (Lado)

Minimises your body profile — useful in weapon defence.

**Setup:**
- Body turned sideways to the opponent
- Weapon-side leg forward
- Non-weapon hand tucked behind

**Key points:**

- Your side profile is a much smaller target than your front
- Limits your attacking angles — use situationally

## Footwork Patterns

### Triangle Step (Triángulo)

The foundational movement pattern in Kali. You move to three points of an imaginary triangle to evade and reposition.

```
         [Point A]  ← Strike comes in
        /         \
  [Point B]    [Point C]
(Step outside)  (Step inside)
```

- **Step outside** (Point B): move to the weapon side of the attacker
- **Step inside** (Point C): move to the non-weapon side

!!! tip
    Always move **off the line of attack** first. Never step straight back.

---

### Passing Step

A linear shuffle step used to quickly close or open distance.

- Lead foot steps first in the direction of travel
- Rear foot follows — **do not cross your feet**
- Stay low; don't bob up and down

---

### Replacement Step (Hakbang Palit)

Swap your lead and rear foot positions in a single motion. Used to shift your angle rapidly.

---

## Footwork Drills

=== "Solo"

    1. Mark a triangle on the floor with tape (~shoulder-width sides)
    2. Start at any point
    3. Call out "outside" or "inside" and step accordingly
    4. 3 × 2 minutes, smooth and controlled
    5. Progress: add a stick strike on each step

=== "Partner"

    1. Partner throws a slow feed (angle 1 strike)
    2. You triangle-step to either side and counter
    3. Switch roles every 10 reps
    4. Progress: partner varies the angle each rep

---

!!! danger "Common mistakes"
    - **Crossing your feet** — instantly kills your balance
    - **Standing flat-footed** — you'll be too slow to react
    - **Leaning back** to avoid a strike instead of moving your feet
