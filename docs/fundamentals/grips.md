# Weapon Grips

How you hold the weapon determines power, speed, control, and your ability to flow into the next movement. A bad grip will sabotage even a technically correct strike.

---

## Standard Grip (Abierta / Open Grip)

The most common grip for single stick work.

**How to grip:**

1. Hold the stick in your dominant hand
2. Position the butt of the stick **flush with the heel of your palm** — about 1–2 cm of stick protruding beyond your hand (the *punyo*)
3. Wrap all four fingers firmly around the stick
4. Thumb rests naturally on the side — **do not wrap thumb over the top**
5. Grip pressure: firm but not death-grip tight (~70% squeeze)

!!! tip "The punyo (butt strike)"
    Letting the butt protrude slightly lets you use the *punyo* as a short-range weapon — a backward hammerfist with the butt end. It also prevents the stick from being stripped from your hand.

## Alive Hand (Live Hand / Kamay)

Your non-weapon hand is **never passive** in Kali. It is called the *alive hand* or *live hand*.

**Roles of the alive hand:**

- **Check** — intercept or redirect the opponent's weapon hand
- **Trap** — pin or immobilise the weapon arm
- **Strike** — open-palm slap, hammer fist, elbow
- **Guard** — shield the face or body

**Key drill:** Practice slow-motion sinawali paying attention to where your alive hand is at every moment. It should never just hang there.

---

## Punyo Grip

A variation where you choke up on the stick, holding it in the middle section, with the butt end prominent.

**When to use:**
- Close range / grappling range
- When you need more control than power

---

## Grip Pressure

| Phase | Pressure |
|---|---|
| At rest / guard | Light (40%) — stays loose and fast |
| On impact | Firm (90%) — locks on contact |
| Follow-through | Relaxes immediately back to light |

!!! warning "Death grip"
    Squeezing the stick at 100% throughout a drill will exhaust your forearm within minutes and slow your strikes. Grip peaks only on impact.

---

## Grip Switching Drills

=== "Solo"
    1. Hold stick in dominant hand, standard grip
    2. Toss the stick from one hand to the other using a wrist flick
    3. Catch in standard grip on the other hand
    4. 3 × 1 minute each direction

=== "With wall target"
    1. Strike a wall pad with angle 1 (forehand diagonal)
    2. On retraction, switch hands
    3. Strike angle 2 (backhand diagonal) with opposite hand
    4. Builds ambidextrous grip confidence
