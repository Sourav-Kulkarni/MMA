# Conditioning

Physical conditioning supports your technique. Weak forearms, a stiff shoulder, or poor cardio will undermine even perfect form under pressure.

| Page | Focus |
|---|---|
| [Warm-Up](warmup.md) | Pre-training mobility and activation |
| [Strength & Power](strength.md) | Grip, forearm, shoulder, and rotational power |
| [Recovery](recovery.md) | Cool-down, stretching, injury prevention |

---

!!! note "Conditioning is secondary to technique — until it isn't"
    For beginners: focus on technique. As you advance, physical limitations (grip endurance, shoulder mobility) will start to cap your growth. Address them proactively.
