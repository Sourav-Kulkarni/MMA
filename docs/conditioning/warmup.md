# Warm-Up Routine

**Duration:** 10–15 minutes  
**Goal:** Raise core temperature, mobilise the joints used in Kali, and activate the nervous system.

---

## The Routine

### 1. General Warm-Up (3 min)

- Light jogging / jumping jacks / skipping in place
- Goal: break a light sweat, heart rate to ~120 BPM

---

### 2. Wrist Circles (1 min)

Kali places enormous stress on the wrists. Warm them up first.

- Extend both arms forward, palms down
- Make slow full circles — 10 inward, 10 outward
- Progress to holding a stick and making the same circles

---

### 3. Shoulder Rolls & Arm Circles (1 min)

- 10 forward shoulder rolls, 10 backward
- 10 small arm circles forward, 10 backward
- 10 big arm circles forward, 10 backward

---

### 4. Hip Circles (30 sec)

- Hands on hips, feet shoulder-width
- Large, slow circles at the hip — 10 each direction

---

### 5. Leg Swings (1 min)

- Hold a wall or partner for balance
- 10 forward-backward leg swings per leg
- 10 lateral leg swings per leg

---

### 6. Deep Lunge with Rotation (1 min)

- Step into a deep lunge
- Twist torso toward the lead leg, reaching overhead arm to the sky
- 5 per side, hold each for 3–4 seconds

---

### 7. Shadow Sinawali — Slow (3 min)

- Heaven Six in the air, no power
- Focus on full range of motion — let the shoulders open
- This transitions your brain from "normal mode" to "training mode"

---

| # | Exercise | Duration |

---

!!! warning "Never skip the wrist warm-up"
    Kali practitioners are at higher risk for wrist and forearm repetitive strain. Cold wrists + high-velocity striking = recipe for injury. 60 seconds of wrist circles takes 60 seconds.
