# Recovery & Injury Prevention

Kali is hard on the wrists, elbows, and shoulders. Build recovery habits from day one.

---

## Post-Training Cool-Down (10 min)

### 1. Wrist Stretches (2 min)

- **Extensor stretch:** Arm extended, palm facing down. Use other hand to gently pull fingers downward. 30 sec per wrist.
- **Flexor stretch:** Arm extended, palm facing up. Use other hand to gently pull fingers backward. 30 sec per wrist.

### 2. Forearm Cross-Friction Massage (2 min)

- Use thumb to apply firm cross-grain pressure along the forearm muscles
- 1 minute per arm
- Helps break up adhesions from high-volume striking

### 3. Shoulder Cross-Body Stretch (1 min)

- Pull one arm across your chest with the other
- 30 sec per side

### 4. Thoracic Spine Rotation (2 min)

- Sit cross-legged, hands behind head
- Rotate torso left and right slowly — 10 reps each direction

### 5. Hip Flexor Stretch (2 min)

- Low lunge position
- Drop the back knee, push hips forward
- 1 minute per side

## Common Injuries & Prevention

| Injury | Why It Happens | Prevention |
|---|---|---|
| Wrist tendinitis | High volume striking without warm-up | Never skip wrist warm-up; wrist roller conditioning |
| Tennis elbow (lateral epicondylitis) | Overuse, poor grip mechanics | Check grip pressure; forearm strengthening |
| Shoulder impingement | Overhead striking without mobility | Face pulls, shoulder mobility work |
| Bruised forearms | Normal from partner drills | Train with proper gear; accept it as part of Kali |

---

## Sleep & Nutrition

No stretching routine compensates for bad sleep or nutrition.

- **Sleep:** 7–9 hours. Skill consolidation and tissue repair both happen overnight.
- **Protein:** ~1.6g per kg bodyweight daily supports muscle repair from high-volume training.
- **Hydration:** Dehydrated muscle is stiffer and more injury-prone.

---

!!! danger "When to stop training and see a doctor"
    - Sharp joint pain (not muscle soreness) during or after training
    - Numbness or tingling in the hands or fingers (nerve involvement)
    - Pain that persists more than 3 days after training
    - Any swelling that doesn't resolve with 24 hours of rest + ice

    DOMS (delayed onset muscle soreness) is normal. Sharp joint pain is not. Know the difference.
