## What is Kali?

**Kali** (also called **Arnis** or **Eskrima**) is the national martial art of the Philippines. It is a weapon-based system — primarily stick and blade — that also encompasses empty-hand combat. What makes Kali unique:

- Weapon training comes **first**, transferring naturally to open-hand fighting
- Strong emphasis on **flow**, angles of attack, and defanging the snake (disarming)
- Highly practical: every movement has direct application
- Teaches you to deal with **armed opponents** from day one

!!! tip "Learning philosophy"
    *"Slow is smooth. Smooth is fast."* — Focus on form before speed. A sloppy fast strike is less effective than a precise slow one. Film yourself when possible.

!!! warning "Safety first"
    Always gear up for partner work. Rattan sticks, padded gloves, and eye protection are non-negotiable during live sparring.
